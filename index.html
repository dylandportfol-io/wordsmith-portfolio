The Frontier: Operational Reality in Legal AI

Deploying Wordsmith in regulated environments requires absolute candour regarding machine capabilities versus human statutory obligations.

● What Wordsmith AI Delivers Today

  • Cross-System Triage & Orchestration: Reaches across inboxes, Jira, and core registries via MCP without storing underlying customer payload data locally.
  • Statutory Clock & SLA Normalisation: Automatically parses unstructured intake to calculate and initiate strict deadlines (e.g., GDPR 72-hr notifications, DISP Day 3 / Week 8 clocks).
  • Playbook & Blueprint Adherence: Executes deterministic, multi-branch workflows against corporate knowledge repositories (ISO 27001 policies, Consumer Duty matrices, RTS 22 rules).
  • First-Draft & Evidence Synthesis: Assembles complex Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) packs, reverse-solicitation memos, and audit registers in plain, standard-compliant English.

● What AI Cannot Do (Current Frontiers)

  • Unsupervised Regulatory Discretion: Wordsmith cannot replace senior legal or MLRO liability. It surfaces defensible rationale; the legal sign-off remains with qualified counsel.
  • Autonomous State-Changing Actions: High-liability external dispatches (e.g., notifying the ICO under Article 33, STOR filings to the FCA, ARM submissions) must never bypass a Human-in-the-Loop review gate.
  • Handling Ambiguity Without Grounding: An agent cannot extrapolate statutory positions in an information vacuum. If internal policy or precedent is absent from the Repository, hallucination risk spikes.
  • Subjective Risk Appetite Calibration: AI cannot determine an enterprise's commercial tolerance for borderline sanctions nexus or complex multi-jurisdictional licensing exemptions without codified boundaries.
